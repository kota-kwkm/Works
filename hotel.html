Hotel a la carte

Tokyo, Japan
Competition / Hotel
Design — Group

2022

Hotel a la carte stands on the site of the former Nakagin Capsule Tower. Here, functions that were previously housed in a single hotel room are now scattered throughout the building. The room next door might be a living room, the one opposite a gallery.

Tokyo is a fluid city, full of diverse activities and changing daily. By rethinking established building types, we aimed to create a new hotel connected to the city.

Hotel a la carteは中銀カプセルタワーの跡地に建つ。かつて一つの部屋に収まっていた機能が建物全体に散在し、隣の部屋はリビング、向かいの部屋はギャラリーかもしれない。東京という流動的な都市と繋がる新しいホテルのあり方を目指した。
